# Informed Consent Form: Context Intelligence Framework User Study

## Project Title
Context Intelligence Framework: Grounded Architectural Decision Tracking and Drift Prevention in Software Evolution

## Principal Investigator & Course Context
Software Engineering Project Evaluation (Course: UCS503).

---

## 1. Study Purpose
You are invited to participate in a research study evaluating software engineering developer tooling for recovering architectural decision rationale and tracing design history in software repositories.

---

## 2. Participant Tasks & Time Commitment
- If you agree to participate, you will be asked to complete two short software comprehension tasks requiring you to discover why specific architectural choices were made in an open repository.
- One task will use standard repository search tools (git log, repository search), and one will use the Context Intelligence Framework UI.
- The entire session will take approximately **20 to 25 minutes**.

---

## 3. Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw
- Your participation in this study is **entirely voluntary**.
- You may decline to answer any specific question or choose to withdraw from the study at any time without penalty or consequence.

---

## 4. Anonymity & Data Confidentiality
- **No personally identifiable information** (such as your name, student ID, or contact email) will be linked to your responses.
- You will be assigned a random participant identifier (e.g., `P01`, `P02`).
- All collected metrics (completion time, answer accuracy, Likert confidence ratings) will be aggregated and analyzed anonymously.
- The recorded data will be used solely for academic coursework and project evaluation.

---

## 5. Consent Acknowledgement
By proceeding with the study, you acknowledge that:
1. You have read and understood the information provided above.
2. You have had an opportunity to ask questions, and any questions have been answered to your satisfaction.
3. You freely consent to participate in this study.
